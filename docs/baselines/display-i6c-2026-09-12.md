Date: 2026-09-12. Development resumed by the owner after the documentation pause.
Scope: independent software/USB preparation; joint physical acceptance V3 open.

## Owner observations and setup

The owner reported improved appearance and that BOOT changes pages. During a
subsequent manually darkened I6a check, they confirmed one press wakes the same
page and a second changes it. This closes those specific physical observations;
it does not claim complete RGBW/border, rapid-button, RF or mounted-collar checks.

COM6 still reported I6a pages and GPS `no-data`, RX/overflow 0. No connection of
GPS or strips was reported. An eight-second preflight contained no SYS record;
it is device-state evidence only. The 60.016-second manual-wake capture sent
only `b`, then observed physical input; no injected `n`. It ended at 14 clicks,
heap 160,852 bytes in three SYS samples. One malformed joined record and 6,512
additional log-drop bytes were observed; this is not a zero-drop benchmark.

## Change and software checks

`InactivityTimer` is a value-only monotonic/latching timer, disabled on boot.
Stage 3 offers `i/o` to opt into 30 s or disable it without persistence. Light
expiration cannot be undone by a redraw; a short release renews interaction and
wakes without navigation. Product starts with the previous always-on policy.
Implementation/commands: [I6c guide](../../Platformio/Dog-RGB/docs/display-i6c.md).

- Firmware host suite: **147/147**, 23.752 s, including a new log-summary case.
- Shared renderer: **4/4 CTest**, 0.17 s in the final local run; real service
  tests include twenty timeout/wake cycles across two pages and clock boundaries.
- The first test run exposed a fixture ordering error: the existing GPS-wrap
  check was left on Connection. Restoring Activity before that check fixed the
  test; no production behavior was changed to satisfy it.
- Nine rendered PNG hashes match I6a. No new visual assets or dependencies.

## USB inactivity observation

The final capture lasted **190.031 s**, with 323 LCD, 321 GPS and six SYS
records (SYS span 150 s). All thirteen reported state transitions matched the
planned sequence: default-off timer, enable, expire/wake Activity, navigate,
expire/wake Connection, disable for more than 30 s, re-enable/expire, disable
while dark, wake and return to Activity. It ended at `timeouts=3`, `clicks=4`,
light on and timer disabled. These four clicks were serial-injected `n`, not
physical BOOT observations. Exact steps/times are retained with the raw capture.

No malformed records, reset/fatal markers or GPS-clock regressions were observed.
Heap stayed 160,604 bytes in the six SYS samples; lifetime minimum stayed
160,224. Log drops stayed 7,326 with no increase during this window. GPS remained
`no-data`, RX/overflow 0; rendering fixtures did not become real recorded data.
Maximum aggregate display service time was **44,063 µs**, below 50 ms in this
USB window. The mixed navigation p95 histogram upper bound reached 50,000 µs;
it is not a normal-update p95 result or a visible-response measurement.
Before navigation, the samples at 20–70 s of device time reported a normal-draw
maximum of 14,693 µs and p95 upper bound 20,000 µs.
Final LVGL free pool was 42,376 bytes, largest block 42,360.

The short USB run is not endurance or joint-load acceptance. In a separate
final observation, only `a/i` were sent to select Activity and enable the timer;
the owner then confirmed automatic darkness and correct physical wake/navigation.
This confirmation is distinct from the first manual-darkness check and the
four injected `n` events in the timed run. The 30 s experiment is left enabled
for this boot; reset disables it. Final passive-log details follow below.

## Remaining acceptance

The experiment does not close GNSS/LED/HTTP joint load, portable power or runtime.
No animation, new rest classifier, battery estimate or automatic product timeout
is introduced. The [plan](../PLANS/2026-09-12_display-incremental-delivery.md)
keeps V2/V3 ahead of acceptance; I6c preparation is not acceptance of that gate.

Local logs, generated renderer manifest and images are under ignored
`Platformio/Dog-RGB/artifacts/display-i6c/`. Historical I6a captures, binaries and
baseline are preserved.
